SEPSMANAGEMENT

A simple guide to shipping from Miami to St. Eustatius.

1

Register for eServices

Go to tropical.com and create your eServices account.

2

Activate your account

Open the email from noreply@okta.com and click the activation link.

3

Log in to your account

Sign in to eServices using your email and password.

4

Find your TropBox number

In eServices, go to My Account → Addresses and TropBox. Copy your TropBox number.

6

Upload your invoices

Once your cargo is delivered and appears in eServices, upload an invoice for every shipment by the cutoff below.

7

Check the EEI requirement

If any single invoice exceeds US$2,500, contact Mr. Ryan Rouse before the deadline for guidance on EEI filing.
